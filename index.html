The Problem

The knowledge is there.
Finding it is the problem.

Design Release Engineers in Automobile, Consumer Electronics, Aerospace, etc. spend a significant part of every day on indirect work — hunting across PLM systems, shared drives, legacy PDFs, and tribal memory for knowledge that already exists somewhere in the organization, while spinning their CAD to understand which information is relevant to their parts.

The cost is not just time. A single late-stage design change in automotive can cost millions in tooling and program delay. When institutional knowledge walks out the door, those costs multiply across every future program.

The Product

Contextual design intelligence, inside your CAD environment.

DesignFlowAI integrates directly into CAD, enabling engineers to retrieve general and company-specific engineering knowledge via natural language. It connects that knowledge to CAD geometry, providing actionable design insights in context. Engineers spend less time searching and more time on actual engineering design.

The system combines a general engineering layer (industry standards, DFM rules, material properties) with a customer-specific layer (company lessons learned, internal guidelines, proprietary standards).
