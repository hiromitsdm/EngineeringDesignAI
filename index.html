The Problem

The knowledge is there.
Finding it is the problem.

Design Release Engineers in Automobile, Consumer Electronics, Aerospace, etc. spend a significant part of every day on indirect work — hunting across PLM systems, shared drives, legacy PDFs, and tribal memory for knowledge that already exists somewhere in the organization, while spinning their CAD to understand which information is relevant to their parts.

The cost is not just time. For example, a single late-stage design change in automotive can cost millions in tooling and program delay. When institutional knowledge walks out the door, those costs multiply across every future program.

The Product

Contextual design intelligence, inside your CAD environment.

Metis integrates directly into CAD, enabling engineers to retrieve general and company-specific engineering knowledge via natural language. It connects that knowledge to CAD geometry, providing actionable design insights in context. Engineers spend less time searching and more time on actual engineering design.

The system combines a general engineering layer (industry standards, DFM rules, material properties) with a customer-specific layer (company lessons learned, internal guidelines, proprietary standards).

The Vision

Institutional memory
that compounds.
From explicit to structured to tacit.

Every engineer accumulates judgment that never makes it into a document — the reason a fillet radius got bumped up after a field failure, the supplier quirk that changes a tolerance, the fix that only lives in one person's head. Metis captures that tacit knowledge directly from engineers, including your most experienced ones, as they work in CAD, and turns it into structured, retrievable knowledge the moment it's created.

Over time, this becomes an institutional memory that compounds — accumulating with every design decision and passing that expertise on to the next generation of engineers, so it stays with the company even as people move on.
